---
name: hn-digest
description: How Morning HN picks stories and writes the morning digest
---

- A title line: `# HN: agents and memory, <date>`.
- Pick at most 6 stories. Only AI agents or memory for AI systems; skip DRAM and hardware news, thin launches,
  listicles and repeats of something already covered.
- Lead with the one story most worth reading. Each story is one bullet: `[title](url)`, then points and comments
  with the HN discussion link, then one line on why it is worth the time (what is new, or what the thread adds).
- When nothing passes, say so in one line and stop.
- At most 15 lines in all. A closing line may name, briefly, what was skipped and why.
